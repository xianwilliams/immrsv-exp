# Implementation brief

The user superseded the image phase and requested the complete Next.js website. Latest palette is black and white with restrained warm-red accents from the original site; no navy. The supplied logo is preserved in monochrome. Original photographs and footage retain their color. No AI renders are used in the website.

Follow the original sequence and references: a white logo intro animates into a photographic wordmark connected to edge-to-edge imagery, compact monospaced navigation and WORK WITH US at upper right; client logo wall; centered services with brief descriptions and distinct visuals; BUILDING EXPERIENCES on white with black type and a spatial ribbon that resolves into clickable projects; full-image testimonial carousel with supplied exact reviews; rounded split contact popup with footage and large logo left, form right.

Latest refinements: the hero uses seven different projects in a continuous right-to-left photo carousel with no image gaps. Source aspect ratios preserve the full image height. The wordmark is a clean SVG with straight edges and smooth curves, shared by the intro and hero mask. Services use the supplied React Bits accordion treatment with guided “When to call” and “What we handle” copy. Every project popup has an autoplay image accordion and click-to-enlarge viewer. Every project also has a complete gallery page at `/work/[slug]`, with a centered full logo, shared contact popup, and documented contribution breakdown. Pendulum and Coffee N Clothes case-study references inform the image-first page structure.

Match the HERO EXAMPLE.mov and EXAMPLE HERO.png proportions. No alternate editorial split hero, invented headline, navy stage, boxed hero image, or decorative generated imagery. PROJECTS EXAMPLE.mov is a sequence: type separates, ribbon curves across space, panels settle into a usable gallery. Nick's brief determines copy: an adaptable production resource for agencies, internal teams and creative partners, from a small sprint to complete execution. Roles are precise participation credits, not ownership of every project.

Journey and feeling: recognition in the logo reveal; confidence at client proof; relief at flexible services; curiosity and delight as the work ribbon opens; trust through reviews; readiness in the inquiry. Peak: the work curves around the visitor and settles into projects they can explore. No dead pinned scroll. Motion families: mask reveal, continuous hero pan, short stagger, image accordion expansion, spatial ribbon transformation, background carousel dissolve. Mobile and reduced-motion preserve complete content and controls without scroll pinning. Reduced motion disables automatic media movement.

Implementation bundles: hero and structure first, section interactions and inquiry second, desktop/mobile/keyboard/reduced-motion verification third. User explicitly authorized the whole build; no additional planning gate. Local workspace implementation; no repository creation or commits.

Latest refinements: navigation now has a persistent light/dark toggle. Services have an all-caps heading on the left, full-panel photo accordion on the right, thin uppercase IBM Plex Mono service titles with a separator and individual icons below, and six-second autoplay with a two-pixel orange-red progress accent. A pause control and reduced-motion support remain available. The old service taglines are removed. The closing headline is uppercase.
